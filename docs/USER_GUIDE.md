# Swarsetu — User Guide

App link: https://claude.ai/artifact/PR1cMZBjS2hc13YcXh9b5m

The same guide is inside the app under **Help & FAQ**, with search.

## Step-by-step guides

### Start: one link for everyone

_For: Everyone_

1. Open the Swarsetu link.
2. To start a new college, choose New User → Register a new college. You become its Admin and get a college code (for example RAM-7K2Q).
3. Faculty and students choose New User → Join my college, pick the college from the list or type the code, and send a join request.
4. Everyone checks their email and mobile with a 6-digit code.
5. The Admin approves join requests in User Management. Approved people sign in with their username, email or mobile and the app opens their own college with the right screens for their role.

### Set up your college (Admin)

_For: Admin_

1. Open College Profile. Add the address, phone, email and the number of students per Online batch.
2. Choose whether sign-ups need your approval, whether a Gmail address is required, and when holiday reminders go out.
3. Add fee payment details: payee name, UPI ID, mobile linked to payment apps, bank account and IFSC. Upload your college scanner if you have one — the app checks it pays your UPI ID.
4. Open Roles & Permissions to decide what Faculty and Students can do.
5. Add courses, then students (or load them in Import & Export).

### Approve faculty and students

_For: Admin_

1. Open User Management. Join requests are at the top.
2. Check the name, email and mobile (both were verified with a code).
3. Choose the role to approve them as. For students, pick their enrolment from the list — the app suggests one when the email or mobile matches.
4. Press Approve. They get a welcome message and can sign in.
5. Press Reject if you do not know them; they see your reason when they try to sign in.

### Reset someone's password (Admin)

_For: Admin_

1. Open User Management and press Reset password on their row.
2. Enter your own password to confirm.
3. Give them the temporary password in person or by phone. They must choose their own when they sign in.

### Forgot my password

_For: Everyone_

1. On the sign-in page, press "Forgot username or password?".
2. Enter your username, email or mobile number.
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your email and mobile. Codes last 10 minutes.
4. Choose a new password with 8+ characters, upper and lower case, a number and a symbol.

### Edit my profile

_For: Everyone_

1. Press your name at the bottom of the menu (or open My Profile).
2. Change your name, username, email, mobile, city or country, then press Save my details.
3. If you changed your email or mobile, enter the code sent to the new one.
4. To change your password, press Change password and enter your current one first.

### Mark attendance (Faculty)

_For: Admin, Faculty_

1. Open Attendance → Mark Attendance. The Attendance menu opens when you click it.
2. Choose the batch and month (or a month range).
3. Pick the status to apply (Present, Late, Absent, Leave, Holiday…), then tap the cells.
4. Press Save attendance. Saved entries can be corrected later; each change records who made it.
5. Check "Absence notices from students" below the register and press Mark as leave for students who told you in advance.

### Tell my teacher I can't attend (Student)

_For: Student_

1. Open My Attendance.
2. Under "Can't attend a class?", choose the class and a reason.
3. Press Notify teacher before the class starts. Your teacher gets a message and can mark it as leave.
4. You can cancel the notice before the class if your plans change.

### Pay fees (Student)

_For: Student_

1. Open My Fees. Your pending dues are shown at the top.
2. Choose the month and amount. Scan the QR code with Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM, or copy the UPI ID or bank details.
3. Before paying, check that your app shows the college's payee name.
4. After paying, enter the 12-digit UPI transaction reference (UTR) and press Send for verification.
5. When the college verifies it, your dues update and you get a message in your Inbox.

### Verify student payments (Faculty / Admin)

_For: Admin, Faculty_

1. Open Fee Payments. "Payments waiting for verification" lists what students sent.
2. Check your bank or UPI app that the amount with that reference arrived.
3. Press Verify to add it to the student's payments, or Reject with a reason.

### Holidays and automatic reminders

_For: Admin, Faculty, Student_

1. Admins add holidays and vacations in the College Calendar.
2. Before each one starts (1 day before by default — change it in College Profile), everyone it applies to gets a reminder in their Inbox.
3. If SMS/email reminders are switched on, they are listed in Notifications (sending is simulated in this prototype).

### Load existing data (Import)

_For: Admin_

1. Open Import & Export → Upload & import.
2. Choose the data type and upload Excel, CSV, text, a PDF or a photo.
3. Check what was read, match each field to a column, review the rows, then press Import. Undo is in Import history.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is the Admin?

The person who registers a college is its first Admin. An Admin can make others Admins when approving a join request or in User Management. Every college always keeps at least one active Admin.

### How does the app know which college I belong to?

Your account belongs to one college. When you sign in, the app opens that college and only its data. Faculty and students choose their college (or enter its code) when they sign up.

### What is the college code?

A unique code for each college, such as RAM-7K2Q. It is shown on the College Profile and in My Profile. Share it with faculty and students so they join the right college. Two colleges cannot register the same name.

### Can I sign in with my mobile number or Gmail?

Yes. Sign in with your username, your email or your 10-digit mobile number. Your username itself can also be a name, your mobile or your Gmail.

### Why can't I sign in after registering?

Your college Admin has to approve your join request first. You will see a message saying it is waiting. Ask your college office if it takes long.

### My account is locked

After 5 wrong passwords an account is locked for 15 minutes. Use "Forgot password" to reset it straight away, or ask your Admin.

### I changed my phone number

Open My Profile, enter the new number and save. A code is sent to the new number to confirm it.

### Can I move to a different college?

Yes. In My Profile choose the college and press Request to join. You are signed out until its Admin approves you. Your old records stay with your old college.

### Is it safe to pay through the app?

The app never takes card details, UPI PINs or OTPs. You pay in your own UPI or banking app. Always check that the payee name in your app matches the one shown. If the college changed its payment details recently, the app warns you for 14 days.

### I paid but my dues did not change

Payments are added once the college checks the money arrived. Your submission shows "Waiting for verification" until then. If it is rejected, the reason is shown.

### Why was my transaction reference refused?

A UPI reference (UTR) is 12 digits. Each reference can be sent only once, to stop duplicates.

### Who can change the college bank or UPI details?

Only Admins, and only after re-entering their password. Every change is logged and all Admins are notified.

### When do holiday reminders go out?

By default one day before each holiday or vacation. Admins can change this to 2, 3 or 7 days, or turn it off, in College Profile. Reminders are created when anyone from the college opens the app on or after that day.

### Why can't I see a screen?

Screens follow your role's permissions. Ask your Admin to enable it in Roles & Permissions.

### What is simulated in this version?

Sending SMS and email (verification codes, reminders, notifications) is simulated: the codes are shown on screen. Real sending needs an SMS/email service. Full protection between colleges also needs a server; see the Security notes.

### Security notes

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Accounts lock after repeated wrong passwords and sign out after 30 minutes idle. New sign-ups verify email and mobile and need approval. Payment details need a password to change and are checked against the uploaded scanner. Limits of this prototype: data lives in the artifact's shared database, which every signed-in viewer of the link can read, and checks run in the browser. A production version needs a server that enforces college separation and sends real codes.
